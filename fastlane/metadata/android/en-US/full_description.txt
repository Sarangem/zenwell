Zenwell helps you break the cycle of mindless doomscrolling and reduce screentime. Select which apps you want to block(Eg: Instagram, Youtube Shorts) and Zenwell would not allow you open the app, unless you solve some hard math problems or practice breathing.

<B><U>🚀 Key Features</U></B>
• <B>Customizable Schedules:</B> Select specific apps to block and set active windows (e.g., 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM) and specific weekdays.
• <B>Dynamic Unlock Methods:</B> Choose your level of friction to regain focus:
	• <B>Strict Block:</B> No entry allowed during restricted hours.
    • <B>Timer:</B> Wait for a set countdown before the app opens.
    • <B>Breathing Timer:</B> A guided pause to calm the "scroll reflex."
    • <B>Math Problems:</B> Solve math equations or multiplication tables.
    • <B>Intentional Typing:</B> Type a custom message to proceed.
• <B>Pomodoro Mode:</B> Integrated focus sessions to balance work and rest.
• <B>Privacy First:</B> No Internet permission. Your data stays on your device.

This project is in active development and various bugs are expected. Please report any issues at GitHub.

<B> Permissions Required</B>
• <B>Accessibility Service:</B> To run in background and check for distracting apps to block them.
• <B>Usage Statistics (Optional):</B> To check your smartphone usage statistics.
